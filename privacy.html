Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 1, 2026 Last Updated: July 28, 2026 Contact: jones@sabledreams.com

Welcome to Sable Dreams. Sable Dreams is a digital journaling and wellness application created by Sable Dreams LLC, a Georgia limited liability company. We are deeply committed to your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. By downloading, accessing, or using the Sable Dreams app or website (sabledreams.com), you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Our commitment: We do not collect, read, store, or share your personal journal entries, mood logs, AI conversations, or saved book lists. Your inner life stays yours.

01 Information We Collect

Sable Dreams collects only the minimum information necessary to operate and manage the app. We collect three categories of data:

A. Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your password (stored in encrypted form — we never see your actual password)

This information is used solely to create and manage your account, authenticate your identity, and communicate essential service updates.

B. Subscription Information

When you subscribe to a paid plan, we collect:

  • Subscription tier (free or paid)
  • Subscription status (active, expired, cancelled)
  • Billing date and renewal information

Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card number or full payment details.

C. App Usage Timestamps & Feature Metrics

To manage the business operations and performance of the Sable Dreams app, we collect the following anonymized usage data:

  • When the app is opened and closed (session timestamps)
  • General time-of-day usage patterns (e.g., morning, midday, evening)
  • App feature usage metrics — which features are accessed and how frequently (e.g., journal opened, Sable accessed, mood check-in completed, manifestation board viewed)
  • Total number of feature interactions across time periods
  • Subscription feature access patterns (free vs. paid feature usage rates)
Important

We record WHEN and HOW OFTEN features are used — not WHAT you write, say, or save. The content of your journal entries, AI conversations, mood logs, and saved books is never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

This usage data is collected exclusively for internal business management purposes — to understand how the app is performing and how to improve the experience. All data is anonymized and aggregated. It is never linked to your personal identity in our analytics systems and is never shared with or sold to third parties.

02 What We Do NOT Collect

We want to be crystal clear about what we do not collect:

We do NOT collect the content of your journal entries.
We do NOT collect your mood logs or self-care activity details.
We do NOT collect your Sable AI conversations.
We do NOT collect your saved books or Dream Library contents.
We do NOT collect your Manifestation Board images or intentions.
We do NOT sell your personal information to anyone, ever.
We do NOT use your data for advertising or ad targeting.

All personal content you create inside the Sable Dreams app is private to you.

03 How We Use Your Information

The limited information we collect is used only for the following purposes:

  • To create, authenticate, and manage your account
  • To communicate essential service updates, account notices, and support responses
  • To process and manage your subscription status
  • To analyze anonymized, aggregated usage timestamps and feature metrics to understand app performance and improve the user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect the safety of our users and services
04 AI Features — Sable, Your AI Life Coach

Sable Dreams includes an AI-powered life coaching feature called Sable, designed to support your personal growth, journaling practice, and emotional wellbeing.

  • Sable is powered by artificial intelligence and is not a licensed therapist, counselor, psychologist, or medical professional
  • Sable does not provide medical advice, mental health treatment, crisis intervention, or emergency services
  • Your conversations with Sable are processed in real time to generate responses and are not stored on our servers or shared with third parties
  • The "Not medical advice" notice displayed in the app applies to all Sable interactions
In a Crisis?

If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, please contact a licensed professional or call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or 911 immediately.

05 How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

A. Service Providers

  • OpenAI — to power the Sable AI feature (real-time processing only; conversations are not stored)
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — to manage subscription billing
  • Cloud hosting providers — to run app infrastructure

All service providers are required to protect your data and may only use it to perform services on our behalf.

B. Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Sable Dreams LLC or our users.

C. Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your account information may be transferred. We will notify you and provide options regarding your data.

06 Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your account information, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/SSL)
  • Encrypted password storage — we never store your password in plain text
  • Access controls limiting who can access account data
  • Regular security monitoring and reviews

Because your personal content is stored on your device and not transmitted to our servers, it is protected by your device's own security — including your passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.

07 Data Retention
  • Your account information is retained for as long as your account is active
  • Anonymized usage timestamp and feature metric data may be retained in aggregate form for up to 12 months for analytics purposes
  • After account deletion, certain information may be retained for up to 90 days for backup and legal compliance, after which it is permanently deleted

You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting jones@sabledreams.com.

08 Your Rights and Choices
  • Access: request a copy of the account information we hold about you
  • Correction: request that we correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: request that we delete your account and associated data
  • Opt-Out: opt out of non-essential communications at any time
  • Withdraw Consent: withdraw consent for any processing based on your consent
Contact Us

To exercise any of these rights, email us at jones@sabledreams.com. We will respond within 30 days.

09 Children's Privacy

Sable Dreams is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information to us, please contact us at jones@sabledreams.com and we will promptly delete it.

10 Subscriptions and Payments

Sable Dreams offers free and paid subscription tiers at $5.00/month or $45.00/year.

  • All payments are processed by Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android)
  • We do not directly collect or store your payment card information
  • Subscription management and cancellation is handled through your Apple ID or Google Account settings
  • Refunds are subject to Apple App Store or Google Play refund policies

AI-powered features (Sable) are available to paid subscribers only.

11 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • The right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it
  • The right to delete your personal information
  • The right to opt out of the sale of personal information (Note: we do not sell personal information)
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

To submit a California privacy request, email jones@sabledreams.com with the subject line "California Privacy Request."

12 Changes to This Privacy Policy
  • We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy when changes are made
  • We will notify you through the app or by email if changes are material
  • The updated policy will always be available at sabledreams.com/privacy

Your continued use of the app after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
